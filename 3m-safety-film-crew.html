The bead that makes it a system: minimum 3/8 inch of IPA on BOTH the film and the frame for windstorm / impact / forced entry — 1/2 inch for blast mitigation (not counting gaskets or glazing stops).

Checklist — use per window

Installer: ______________________

Before / Prep

  • Assess the glazing stop type. Film must go as far into the glazing channel as possible, with enough spacing between film and glazing bead so slip solution can be removed.
  • Thoroughly clean glazing and frame — remove all foreign matter and contaminants: adhesives, grease, oil, dust, old sealant, glazing compounds.
  • Use the cleaners named in the 3M spec as needed: 3M Citrus Base Cleaner, 3M Adhesive Remover (Citrus Base), 3M Foaming Glass Cleaner, 3M 0000 Super Fine Synthetic Steel Wool for dirty areas. Detergent / soap-and-water alone is NOT recommended for this prep step.
  • Spray glazing bead, glass and frame; wipe with a lint-free cloth; repeat if needed. Scrape the glass with a razor. Final clean, then wipe around the glazing bead and frame to remove all soap and water solution.

Film install

  • Apply the 3M Safety & Security film to the glass.
  • Squeegee firmly to remove as much slip solution as possible — especially at the edges.
  • Edge-dry before IPA: EITHER leave the panels a few weeks to dry properly, OR gently heat and bump the film edges with a hair dryer to speed edge drying.
  • All soap and water is out of the film / glass / glazing channel before IPA goes on.

Gasket

  • With an Olfa knife, trim as much of the rubber gasket as possible without disturbing the window seal or letting rubber fall into the glazing channel. (3M baseline is trimming. Full gasket removal is the Epic variation — only with owner approval on a bid spec.)

IPA bead — the part that makes it a system

  • Tape a 1-inch strip of masking tape on the film, 3/8 inch (9 mm) in from the film edge, all four sides.
  • Tape a 1-inch strip on the frame, 3/8 inch from the trimmed gasket edge. This forms the parallel channel for a uniform bead.
  • Minimum bead overlap confirmed for this job: 3/8 inch on both film and frame (windstorm / impact / forced entry) — 1/2 inch for blast mitigation.
  • Clean the taped channel surface before shooting the bead (rubbing alcohol on a lint-free towel, wipe dry — per current 3M IPA sealant instructions).
  • Apply a triangular bead of IPA. Start in a corner, shoot about 6 inches, then turn the gun and PUSH the bead to the next corner. Pushing ensures penetration and minimizes air gaps. No bubbles, no gaps.
  • Tool / smooth the bead in one continuous stroke, immediately after application, before a skin forms — flat, finished, relatively triangular profile.
  • Remove both masking strips immediately after tooling. Do not let excess adhesive contact film, frame, or flooring — use a drop cloth. Clean smears with 3M Citrus Based Cleaner.

Cure / Leave site

  • Do NOT clean the film / IPA system for at least 36 hours.
  • Full curing / adhesion can take up to 7 days depending on temperature and humidity (full adhesion 7–14 days per the spec table).
  • Film itself needs about 30 days for install moisture to dry thoroughly and lie flat with no moisture dimples.
  • Customer was told the cure times in writing. ☐
  • Photo every finished window: all four bead sides close-up + the full pane. Log room / opening, film type, and bead size used.

Red flags — stop and fix

  • Bead laid over an untrimmed, oily EPDM gasket, with no bond to the frame — that's the shortcut install. It will peel or pull out under impact.
  • Film only, no IPA, on a forced-entry, windstorm, or blast job — that is NOT the 3M system. 3M requires IPA for all break-and-entry, windstorm, blast, and spontaneous-breakage (single-pane tempered) applications.
  • Air gaps, or a pulled (not pushed) bead.
  • Tape left on after tooling.
  • Cleaning the film / IPA before 36 hours.
  • Never tell a customer the film is bulletproof. It is not. It holds broken glass and delays entry.
